Humanoid Robot

Description

A Humanoid Robot is designed to resemble the structure and movement of a human being. It usually consists of a head, torso, arms, and legs, allowing it to perform human-like tasks and interact naturally with people.

Key Features

  • Human-like body structure.
  • Artificial Intelligence based interaction.
  • Voice recognition and speech generation.
  • Facial and object recognition.
  • Walking and object handling capabilities.

Applications

  • Healthcare Assistance
  • Customer Service
  • Education
  • Research and Development
  • Industrial Automation

Specifications

Property Details
